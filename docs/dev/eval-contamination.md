# Eval contamination audit (Phase 4.0)

**Status:** blocking audit. No evaluation result in `docs/` may stand unless it
passes this audit. **Date:** 2026-09-19.

## 1. CARMEN-I: train overlap, demoted

Facts gathered from the primary sources:

- The model card
  ([`BSC-NLP4BIA/bsc-bio-ehr-es-carmen-anon`](https://huggingface.co/BSC-NLP4BIA/bsc-bio-ehr-es-carmen-anon))
  states: *"Model trained on the anonymization part of CARMEN-I."* It does **not**
  publish a train/test split.
- The corpus page (PhysioNet,
  [10.13026/x7ed-9r91](https://physionet.org/content/carmen-i/)) describes
  CARMEN-I as a single resource of **2,000 documents** (1,201 imaging, 617
  discharge, 172 referral, 5 death, 5 progress notes), with 500 documents
  carrying extra clinical-concept annotations. No official train/test/dev
  partition is published on PhysioNet.
- The Scientific Data paper (Lima-López et al., *Sci. Data* 12, 1088, 2025,
  doi:10.1038/s41597-025-05320-1) may document the exact partition, but it is
  behind an access wall and could not be inspected in this environment.
- The repository's previous headline ("CARMEN test, 2,000 documents", F1 0.924,
  leakage 2.5%) evaluates on **the whole corpus**, which is also what the model
  was fine-tuned on.

**Conclusion (per the brief):** the split used for fine-tuning cannot be
determined from the model card or the corpus page. Therefore the CARMEN-I
figure is **demoted** to *"in-distribution, upper bound, possible train
overlap"* and **must not** be used as a headline result. It is marked as an
open question for the authors. The previous `docs/metrics.md` CARMEN numbers
are retired; `docs/metrics.md` is regenerated by `make eval` and no longer
contains them unless a documented clean CARMEN split is provided.

## 2. MEDDOCAN: clean out-of-distribution evaluation

- The model was trained on CARMEN-I only (model card, above). MEDDOCAN
  (Marimon et al., IberLEF 2019) was **not** part of the model's training.
- Local data: `data/meddocan/corpus/` has `train/` (1,000), `dev/` (500) and
  `test/` (500) documents in brat format.
- The `train` partition was used to calibrate the tool (threshold, whitelist,
  regex rules). It is therefore **excluded** from evaluation.
- Clean evaluation set: `dev` + `test` = **1,000 documents**.

## 3. Decisions

1. Retire CARMEN-I as a headline metric; if referenced, label it
   "in-distribution, upper bound, possible train overlap" and cite the open
   question.
2. All regenerated numbers come from `eval/results/*.json` produced by
   `make eval`. No hand-edited figures in `docs/metrics.md`.
3. BERT-based evaluation requires the gated model
   (`BSC-NLP4BIA/bsc-bio-ehr-es-carmen-anon`, revision
   `83db1112c37c7ef527a9ba6d6b4d1be18b4bca9b`, weights SHA-256
   `883c7c2c63d01da8af3ea12a8b22237f2896b0ce`). Until the model is available
   locally, the harness runs the regex-only baseline and leaves BERT/combined
   tables empty with an explicit TODO.
